The Test Hall

A command-line tool that fuzzes network protocols by generating and sending malformed packets to a target service to identify potential vulnerabilities or crashes. It sends random, mutated, or oversized random byte packets to a named target. The crash test laboratory strikes its subject again and again with blows bent out of shape; this fuzzer does the same to a service, and the damage is the data.

What it does

The fuzzer generates malformed packets and sends them at a target service, watching for the blow the service cannot take: a vulnerability or a crash. Usage, as the README gives it: ./analyze-network-protocol-fuzzer [params]. Its one requirement is pandas>=2.0.0.
